Welcome to iTECH 2026

A focused international forum for computing and technology research.

FEU Alabang, FEU Diliman, and FEU Tech envision themselves at the forefront of research and technology development and seek to foster technology-driven research collaborations with national and international academic partners.

iTECH 2026 provides a venue for exchanging and sharing best practices in the dynamic field of computing and technology. The conference aims to encourage innovative research and technology solutions to relevant computing problems.

The event will be held on November 5, 2026 at the FEU Tech Innovation Center.
